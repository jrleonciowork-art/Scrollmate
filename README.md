# Scrollmate

![Scrollmate - Your soulmate for every scroll you take](public/og.png)

> Your soulmate for every scroll you take.

Scrollmate is an emerging social media management agency that engineers personalized content and community strategies for businesses ready to scale. We go beyond aesthetic feeds to build digital storefronts that put brands on the map and turn followers into loyal customers.

Our team brings nearly ten years of Facebook management experience and a deep background in corporate media buying, combining organic growth, paid media, content, and community management in one focused partnership.

## What we do

- Social media profile optimization and SEO
- Content scheduling and conversion-focused copywriting
- Feed and grid management
- Community management across comments and direct messages
- Custom social media graphics
- Short-form video editing
- Monthly content strategy and performance reporting
- Facebook and Instagram ad strategy, setup, management, and A/B testing

## The Scrollmate system

Our work follows a continuous four-stage system:

1. **The Blueprint** - establish the brand strategy, voice, aesthetic, and content direction.
2. **The Feed** - plan and organize the brand's social presence.
3. **The Build** - create, publish, manage, and strengthen the content ecosystem.
4. **The Launch** - activate the strategy, measure its performance, and feed insights into the next blueprint.

## Packages

### The Optimizer

Designed for businesses that already have content assets and need a stronger, more consistent social presence.

- Facebook and Instagram profile uplift and SEO
- Action-button and Story Highlight organization
- Content scheduling and caption writing using client-provided materials
- Grid management
- Standard comment and DM replies
- Monthly follower growth and reach report

### The Creator

The recommended package for brands that need both management and original content production. It includes everything in The Optimizer, plus:

- A set number of custom promotional or announcement graphics, such as 8-10 Canva graphics per month
- A set number of short-form video edits
- A monthly content strategy calendar presented for client approval before production

### The Accelerator

Built for brands ready to combine organic content with paid growth. It includes everything in The Optimizer and The Creator, plus:

- Facebook and Instagram ad campaign strategy and setup
- Sales-funnel design
- A/B testing of creative and copy variations
- Campaign setup and management

Package inclusions reflect the August 2026 company profile and should be confirmed before use in proposals or contracts.

## Website development

This repository contains Scrollmate's premium, responsive agency website.

### Local development

Requirements: Node.js 22 and pnpm.

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000).

### Production checks

```bash
pnpm lint
pnpm build
```

### Deploy to Vercel

1. Import the GitHub repository in the Vercel dashboard.
2. Keep the detected framework preset as **Next.js**.
3. Keep the project root as `./`.
4. Deploy. No environment variables are required.

Vercel will automatically build new commits pushed to the production branch.
